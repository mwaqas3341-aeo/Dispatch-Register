Privacy Policy

Dispatch Register is an internal record-keeping tool used by officers of the School Education Department, District Layyah, Punjab, Pakistan. Administrator: Muhammad Waqas. Last updated: 31 October 2026.

What we collect

  • Account details: CNIC (used as the login ID), full name, district, tehsil, wing, and the Gmail address the user confirms.
  • Dispatch records: dispatch number and date, category, subject, remarks, school details, selected offices, inquiry and action records entered by the user.
  • Office contacts: office names and official or personal email addresses of recipient offices, entered by users or the administrator.
  • Document details: file name, type, size, upload date and the Google Drive file reference. The files themselves are not stored by us.

Google user data

If a user connects Google Drive, the app asks for three permissions: to see the user's Google email address (to check it matches the Gmail they confirmed), and the drive.file permission, which lets the app create, read and delete only the files and folders the app itself creates. The app cannot see any other file in the user's Google Drive.

  • Uploaded documents are stored in a folder named "Dispatch_Register" in the user's own Google Drive.
  • The Google refresh token is stored encrypted on our server and is never shown to the browser.
  • We use Google data only to provide the document features described here. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or share it with anyone.

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Email

When a dispatch is saved, the report details and its attached file are emailed to the offices selected by the user. Email is delivered through the service provider Brevo. The sender's Gmail is set as the reply-to address.

Where data is kept and who can see it

Records are stored in a Supabase database. Each user can see only their own dispatch records and documents. Administrators manage user accounts and office lists. We do not sell or share personal data for advertising.

Your choices

  • Disconnect Google Drive at any time from the cloud icon in the app, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Disconnecting does not delete your records or your Drive files.
  • Deleting a document in the app moves its file to the Trash of your Google Drive.
  • To correct or delete your account data, contact the administrator.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/mwaqas3341-aeo/Dispatch-Register/issues or contact the administrator through your department.
